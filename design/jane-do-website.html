About me

Numbers in the city,
stars in the wild.

I'm a Tax Senior at Deloitte in New York City, where I help clients navigate the details of tax so they can focus on what they do best. I'm careful, curious, and happiest when a complicated problem finally clicks into place.

I was born and raised in Shenzhen, China, then crossed the Pacific in 2018 to study accounting at Baylor University in Waco, Texas. After a master's year, an internship and a big move, New York became home.

Outside the office you'll find me packing the car for another national park, setting up camp before dark, and trading screens for a sky full of stars. Have a blessed day, everyone.
